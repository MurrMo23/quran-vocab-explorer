Essential Quranic Arabic Words

Allah (اللّٰه) - God, The Creator

Arabic: اللّٰه | Transliteration: Allah | Root: أ ل ه | Frequency: 2699 occurrences

The most fundamental word in Arabic and Islam, referring to the one and only God. Used throughout the Quran as the primary name for the divine being.

Rahmah (رَحْمَة) - Mercy

Arabic: رَحْمَة | Transliteration: rahmah | Root: ر ح م | Frequency: 114 occurrences

Divine mercy and compassion. One of the most important attributes of Allah, mentioned frequently in Quranic verses.

Kitab (كِتَاب) - Book, Scripture

Arabic: كِتَاب | Transliteration: kitab | Root: ك ت ب | Frequency: 261 occurrences

Refers to divine books and scriptures, particularly the Quran. Essential for understanding revelation and guidance.

Iman (آمَنَ) - Faith, Belief

Arabic: آمَنَ | Transliteration: āmana | Root: أ م ن | Frequency: 818 occurrences

The act of believing and having faith. Central concept in Islamic theology representing trust and belief in Allah.

Salah (صَلاة) - Prayer

Arabic: صَلاة | Transliteration: salah | Root: ص ل و | Frequency: 83 occurrences

The Islamic ritual prayer, one of the five pillars of Islam. Essential practice for Muslim worship and devotion.

Yawm (يَوْم) - Day

Arabic: يَوْم | Transliteration: yawm | Root: ي و م | Frequency: 475 occurrences

Day, often referring to the Day of Judgment. Fundamental concept in Islamic eschatology and temporal references.

Jannah (جَنَّة) - Paradise

Arabic: جَنَّة | Transliteration: jannah | Root: ج ن ن | Frequency: 147 occurrences

The Islamic concept of paradise or heaven. Represents the ultimate reward for righteous believers in the afterlife.

Nabiyy (نَبِيّ) - Prophet

Arabic: نَبِيّ | Transliteration: nabiyy | Root: ن ب أ | Frequency: 75 occurrences

A prophet or messenger sent by Allah to guide humanity. Essential concept for understanding Islamic history and guidance.

Taqwa (تَقْوَى) - God-consciousness

Arabic: تَقْوَى | Transliteration: taqwa | Root: و ق ي | Frequency: 151 occurrences

Consciousness of Allah, piety, and righteous conduct. Central virtue in Islamic ethics and spiritual development.

Rasul (رَسُول) - Messenger

Arabic: رَسُول | Transliteration: rasul | Root: ر س ل | Frequency: 235 occurrences

A messenger of Allah, often referring to prophets who brought divine revelation and guidance to their peoples.
