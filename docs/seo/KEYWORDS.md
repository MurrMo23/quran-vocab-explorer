# QuranVocab keyword research (2026-11-09)

Source: DataForSEO (Google Ads search volume US/worldwide/UK/CA/IN/PK/NG/MY, Labs keyword overview/ideas/
ranked keywords for myislam.org, corpus.quran.com, understandquran.com, quranicvocab.com, quranvocab.com;
7 live SERPs). Volumes are 12-month averages (Oct 2025 to Sep 2026). Spend about US$2.61.
Full data: `keywords.csv` (1,549 rows: 110 P1, 555 P2, 884 P3), `ramadan_seasonality.txt`, `multilingual.json`.

Caveats: close variants share one volume; some terms are polluted by other meanings ("adl meaning" =
activities of daily living, "ar rahman" = the composer, "k-t-b"/"s-l-m" acronyms), marked P3 in the CSV.

## Key findings
1. Islamic phrases are the largest untapped cluster (~407k US/mo): inshallah 90.5k, wallahi 74k,
   mashallah 60.5k, bismillah 40.5k, allahu akbar 40.5k, alhamdulillah 33.1k ("<phrase> meaning").
2. Word pages: "<translit> meaning" (113.7k US in sample) and "<English> in arabic" (~99k) beat
   "<translit> meaning in arabic" (2.8k). KD mostly 0-10.
3. Surah pages: "Vocabulary of Surah X" has zero searches. "<surah> in english" 55k US / 509k global,
   "<surah> translation" 34k / 210k. Bare "surah X" (625k US) is mostly full-text intent.
4. Names: bare names (50.9k US) and "ya <name>" ("ya latif" 27.1k global) beat "<name> meaning" (7.3k US).
5. Root queries have almost no volume; roots serve long tail and internal links. /roots hub can take
   "arabic root words" (480 US / 1.9k global, KD 0).
6. The site barely ranks yet (2 US keywords). Google still indexes legacy /word/<n> URLs with old titles.
7. Ramadan demand is for phrases and events (taraweeh, laylatul qadr, "ramadan mubarak meaning"),
   not "ramadan quran plan" (10/mo). Core learning terms lift only 1.2-1.8x in Ramadan.
8. Multilingual: Indonesian first (~215k/mo matched to our templates), then Turkish (~180k).

## Title and H1 patterns
- Word: title `<Translit> Meaning in the Quran: <English> (<Arabic>)`; H1 `<Translit> (<Arabic>): <English>`;
  support line "How to say '<English>' in Arabic".
- Name: title `<Name> Meaning: <English> | 99 Names of Allah (Ya <Name>)`; H1 `<Name> (<Arabic>) Meaning: <English>`;
  list spelling variants.
- Surah: title `Surah <Name> in English: Word-by-Word Meaning & Vocabulary`; H1 `Surah <Name> (<Arabic>): Words & Meaning`;
  common spellings (Yasin/Yaseen, Kahf, Mulk, Fatiha); add ayah-by-ayah English gloss.
- Root: `<Arabic> (<k-t-b>) Root Meaning: <core>`; /roots hub targets "arabic root words".
- Collections: query form first ("Colors in Arabic", "Days of the Week in Arabic", "Family Words in Arabic",
  "Prophets in the Quran: Names in Arabic & English").
- Home: "Learn Quranic Arabic Vocabulary: Understand the Words of the Quran". /names-of-allah: add "Asmaul Husna".

## New pillar / hub pages
1. /phrases + /phrases/<slug> (~407k US): inshallah, wallahi, mashallah, astaghfirullah, assalamu alaikum,
   jazakallah khair, ameen; link the 4 existing phrase word pages. Show the verse + word-by-word breakdown.
   Don't target /word/masha (the verb "to walk").
2. /most-common-words-in-the-quran ("quran words" 1,900, KD 10; weak SERP).
3. /juz-amma-vocabulary ("juz amma" 1,600 / 165k global; "juz 30"; "short surahs").
4. /salah-words ("prayer in arabic" 1,600, CPC $17.95; "what does salah mean").
5. /quran-statistics (~10k US: how many surahs/verses/juz/words, shortest surah).
6. /islamic-terms A-Z glossary ("arabic words" 8,100; "halal meaning in islam" 74k needs a word page).
7. Quran word-by-word landing or retitle /surahs ("quran word by word" 1,900 / 12.1k global).
8. "<X> in the Quran" topic pages (Jesus 2,400, women 720, jinn 390, Mary 390, Moses 320).
Avoid: "how to learn arabic", "arabic alphabet" (off-core), "quranic corpus" (navigational).
