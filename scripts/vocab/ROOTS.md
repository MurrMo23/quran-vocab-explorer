# Root library (/roots, /root/<slug>)

Every Arabic root in the Quran (1,642), with all 4,657 word forms (lemmas) built from them.
Generated by `scripts/vocab/gen-roots.py` into `public/data/roots/` (one JSON per root plus
`index.json`); the pages load that JSON (`src/lib/static-data.tsx`: embedded in prerendered HTML,
fetched on client navigation), so none of it is in the JS bundle.

```
python3 scripts/vocab/gen-roots.py --corpus <corpus dir> --wbw <wbw data.json> \
  --words <site words json> --en <quran_en.json> --glosses scripts/vocab/root-glosses
```

## Sources
- Roots, lemmas, parts of speech, verb forms (I–X), participles, verbal nouns, counts, locations:
  Quranic Arabic Corpus morphology v0.4. A count is word tokens; a token counts once per root.
- Verse text: Tanzil Uthmani (waqf marks kept with the preceding word, so token i = word position i).
- Verse translation: Saheeh International.
- Word-by-word evidence: Quran.com word-by-word English (npm `@kmaslesa/holy-quran-word-by-word-full-data`).

## Meanings (how they were written, Nov 2026)
- A form that is a QuranVocab word uses the site's curated meaning.
- Every other form, and each root's core meaning, was written as a short dictionary meaning from the
  Quran.com word-by-word translations of its occurrences (most frequent first), its verb form and an
  example verse, in 14 review batches (`root-glosses/out00–13.json`). Verbs are "to …", derived forms
  reflect their pattern, participles and verbal nouns are nouns/adjectives.
- 84 items were marked uncertain by the reviewers (the `uncertain` lists in those files): words whose
  Quranic sense is debated or varies by verse. They carry the best-supported meaning.

## Example verses
Each form shows the shortest verse that uses it (earliest on ties), with the word highlighted.
